# What belongs in this backup

**Checked against the Git tree, not just filenames.** The public operation driver, projected fixture, comparison analyzer and the short reports for lean ULP, large-budget retry, producer bounds and unified certificates are already versioned in `021b24818`. The evidence here adds the per-cell records behind those summaries, diagnostics, matrix outcomes, and larger file-backed workloads. Selected source blobs were checked against the complete tree. For example, the unified runner's measured C++ source is blob `b41531cff5339659ae7085076112bc5b1d7890e7` at `cbe774cd9`; the fixture is already at both revisions. Neither is recopied.

**Local adaptations.** The unified study's scheduling, summarization and synthetic tests are retained; instead of shipping duplicate corpus files the runner checks their recorded hashes in the nominated historical source tree. Producer instrumentation is check-only and guards against source drift. The XOR oracle is the local synthetic test with one unused retired type alias removed. The compact decimal and buffer drivers are the original measured Cartesian branches without unrelated strategy code. File-backed GIS and metric-buffer drivers are unchanged. The fixture recipes accept a fresh output directory and do not package source files or unmodified WKT. Other C++ probes are the local experiment sources.

**Not a redistribution grant for input data.**

- No tracker text, comments, attachments, screenshots, reporter coordinates, private reproductions or downloaded issue archives are included. A public issue is not itself evidence of a license to redistribute the attached material. The verdict table contains counts only.
- No map literals, extracted national boundaries, world or road layers, coastline features, transformed WKT or decimal/projected fixture copies are included. In particular, the Greek coastline source has a World Borders attribution. Independent redistribution clearance for the external map and issue data was not established in this review; this is a reason for exclusion, not a determination that all those sources are proprietary. The recipes read an authorized local Geometry source. Re-generated files remain subject to their original terms.
- Installed dependencies, third-party implementation sources, production headers, full source exports, intermediates, binaries, disassembly, profiles, build logs, environment manifests, per-process ledgers and raw coordinate dumps are intentionally absent. Earlier explorations and superseded producer and matrix runs were not combined with qualified measurements.
- No spherical, geographic or GPU campaign is included. The November 19 mixed campaign is represented only by its final Cartesian policy observations and the synthetic XOR oracle. Mapped buffers use a planar metric approximation. The issue ledger's ambiguous and non-Cartesian families were left out.

Re-run artifacts can contain the local user's build environment and fixture coordinates. Generate them outside this directory; they are not part of the public bundle.
